## F174-P02. A polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-polynomial-time-construction-of-strong-thin-trees-September-23-2026/paper.pdf

We give a deterministic polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees. Given a finite <i>k</i>-edge-connected loopless multigraph on at least one vertex, the algorithm constructs a spanning tree meeting every cut in at most a $`C/k`$ fraction of its edges, for a universal constant <i>C</i>. The running time is polynomial in the binary input length, including when parallel-edge multiplicities are encoded in binary.

## F175-P01. Graph Decompositions at the Integral Expectation Threshold

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Graph-Decompositions-at-the-Integral-Expectation-Threshold-October-5-2026/graph-threshold-decompositions.pdf

We prove the graph-decomposition conjecture of Ascoli, He, Park, and Talagrand. Every graph admits a partition into a universally bounded number of fixed edge pieces, each having ordinary containment threshold at most a universal constant times the original graph's integral expectation threshold. The partition is chosen before sampling the random host, and the separate embeddings of the pieces need not agree on shared vertices.

## F175-P02. Integral and fractional expectation thresholds are equivalent

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Integral-and-fractional-expectation-thresholds-are-equivalent-September-23-2026/paper.pdf

We prove Talagrand's conjecture that integral and fractional expectation thresholds are within a universal constant factor, with the same covering budget.

## F175-P03. Talagrand’s discrete-convexity conjecture

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Talagrands-discrete-convexity-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf

We prove Talagrand's discrete-convexity conjecture. There is a universal integer <i>k</i> such that, whenever an arbitrary family has Bernoulli product measure at least $`1-1/k`$, the sets not contained in a union of <i>k</i> members admit a cover of total cost at most 1/2 at the same density.

## F176-P01. The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-second-Kahn-Kalai-conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf

We prove the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture. For every finite simple graph <i>H</i> with <i>h</i> ≥ 1 edges and at most <i>n</i> vertices, the threshold for $`G(n,p)`$ to contain an ordinary copy of <i>H</i> is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, where <i>C</i> is universal. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of <i>H</i> has expected copy count at least one half.

## F177-P01. Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Bounded-degree-coboundary-expanders-in-every-dimension-September-24-2026/paper.pdf

For every integer <i>d</i> ≥ 3, we construct arbitrarily large finite <i>d</i>-dimensional simplicial complexes with uniformly bounded vertex degrees and uniform 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expansion in every degree below <i>d</i>. Together with the known graph and two-dimensional cases, this establishes the existence of bounded-degree 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expanders in every positive dimension.

## F178-P01. Deterministic nonbipartite Ramanujan graphs in every fixed degree

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Deterministic-nonbipartite-Ramanujan-graphs-in-every-fixed-degree-September-23-2026/paper.pdf

For every fixed integer <i>d</i> ≥ 3, we give a deterministic algorithm that constructs a simple nonbipartite <i>d</i>-regular Ramanujan graph on every sufficiently large even number <i>n</i> of vertices. It outputs the full adjacency list in polynomially many bit operations, with an exponent that may depend on <i>d</i>. Every nonconstant adjacency eigenvalue lies strictly between $`-2\sqrt{d-1}`$ and $`2\sqrt{d-1}`$.

## F179-P01. The circulant Hadamard conjecture

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-circulant-Hadamard-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf

We prove the circulant Hadamard conjecture: a real circulant Hadamard matrix has order 1 or 4. As a consequence, Barker sequences of length greater than one exist exactly at lengths 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 13, proving the Barker-sequence conjecture.

## F180-P01. Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs

## F181-P01. A linear cycle-and-edge decomposition of every graph

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-linear-cycle-and-edge-decomposition-of-every-graph-September-24-2026/main.pdf

We prove that every finite simple undirected graph on <i>n</i> vertices has an edge partition into at most $`Cn`$ simple cycles and single edges, for an absolute constant <i>C</i>. This resolves the Erdős–Gallai cycle decomposition conjecture positively.

## F182-P01. A power saving for intersective polynomial differences with an exponent depending only on the degree

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-power-saving-for-intersective-polynomial-differences-with-an-exponent-depending-only-on-the-degree-October-5-2026/power-saving-intersective-polynomial-differences.pdf

An integer polynomial is intersective if it has a root modulo every positive integer. For each degree <i>k</i> ≥ 2, we prove that there is an exponent $`c_k\gt 0`$ such that every set $`A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ whose differences avoid all nonzero values $`h(1),h(2),\ldots`$, where <i>h</i> is an intersective polynomial of degree <i>k</i> with positive leading coefficient, satisfies $`|A|=O_h(N^{1-c_k})`$. The implied constant may depend on <i>h</i>, but the power-saving exponent depends only on its degree.

## F182-P02. A Power Saving for Polynomial Differences at Prime Arguments

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Power-Saving-for-Polynomial-Differences-at-Prime-Arguments-October-5-2026/prime-argument-polynomial-differences.pdf

Let <i>h</i> be a fixed integer polynomial of degree at least two with positive leading coefficient, having a unit root modulo every positive integer. We prove that any set $`A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ whose differences avoid all nonzero values $`h(p)`$ at primes satisfies $`|A|\le C_hN^{1-c_h}`$, where $`c_h\gt 0`$ and $`C_h\ge1`$ depend only on <i>h</i>. Thus the local unit-root condition gives a fixed power saving even when polynomial arguments are restricted to primes. The proof uses the companion zero-free half-plane theorem for Dirichlet <i>L</i>-functions to obtain the required prime-distribution estimates.

## F182-P03. A power saving for square-difference-free sets

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-power-saving-for-square-difference-free-sets-September-24-2026/paper.pdf

We prove that there are absolute constants <i>c</i> &gt; 0 and <i>C</i> &lt; ∞ such that every set $`A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ with no nonzero square difference satisfies $`|A|\le C N^{1-c}`$. This answers the fixed-power question posed by Green and Sawhney.

## F183-P01. A power saving for planar halving lines

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-power-saving-for-planar-halving-lines-September-25-2026/main.pdf

There are absolute constants <i>ε</i> &gt; 0 and <i>C</i> such that every sufficiently large even <i>n</i>-point set in the plane with no three collinear has at most $`Cn^{4/3-\varepsilon}`$ unordered halving pairs. This gives a power saving over the classical $`O(n^{4/3})`$ bound for planar halving lines. The proof is nonquantitative and does not supply explicit constants.

## F184-P01. Correspondence coloring graphs with a forbidden clique

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Correspondence-Coloring-Graphs-with-a-Forbidden-Clique-October-5-2026/correspondence-coloring-forbidden-clique.pdf

For every fixed integer <i>r</i> ≥ 4, we prove that every <i>K</i><sub><i>r</i></sub>-free graph of sufficiently large maximum degree Δ has correspondence chromatic number $`O_r(\Delta/\log\Delta)`$. This resolves the Alon–Krivelevich–Sudakov coloring conjecture in the stronger correspondence-coloring form. The same bound, with a constant depending on <i>F</i>, holds when any fixed graph <i>F</i> is excluded as an ordinary subgraph. Ordinary and list coloring satisfy the same bounds.

## F184-P02. A logarithmic independence bound for clique-free graphs

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Logarithmic-Independence-Bound-for-Clique-Free-Graphs-September-25-2026/paper.pdf

For every fixed integer <i>r</i> ≥ 4, every <i>K</i><sub><i>r</i></sub>-free graph on <i>n</i> vertices with average degree <i>d</i> ≥ 2 has an independent set of size at least $`c_r n\log d/d`$, where $`c_r\gt 0`$ depends only on <i>r</i>. This proves the fixed-clique-size independence conjecture of Ajtai, Erdős, Komlós and Szemerédi.

## F185-P01. A Counterexample to the Infinite Matroid Packing/Covering Conjecture

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Counterexample-to-the-Infinite-Matroid-Packing-Covering-Conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf

We construct in ZFC two self-dual partitional matroids on a countably infinite common ground set that admit neither a packing/covering partition nor an intersection witness. This disproves the unrestricted infinite matroid packing/covering and intersection conjectures and answers Joó's question for two partitional matroids negatively. The examples are neither finitary nor cofinitary.

## F186-P01. A uniform influence bound for hypergraph properties

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-uniform-influence-bound-for-hypergraph-properties-October-5-2026/hypergraph-influences.pdf

For every fixed integer <i>r</i> ≥ 3, we prove that every relabeling-invariant Boolean property of simple <i>r</i>-uniform hypergraphs on <i>n</i> vertices satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits _p(f)\le C_r I_p(f)/(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}`$. The constant depends only on <i>r</i>, and the bound holds uniformly for all $`0\lt p\lt 1`$ without a monotonicity assumption. For increasing properties, it gives the corresponding threshold-width bound with exponent $`r/(r-1)`$, proving the hypergraph threshold-width conjecture of Friedgut and Kalai.

## F186-P02. A Sharp Threshold Bound for Monotone Graph Properties

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Sharp-Threshold-Bound-for-Monotone-Graph-Properties-September-25-2026/paper.pdf

We prove the Friedgut–Kalai sharp-threshold conjecture. For every integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2, every nontrivial increasing family of graphs on <i>n</i> vertices invariant under all vertex permutations, and every $`0\lt \varepsilon\lt 1/2`$, the edge probabilities at which its probability equals <i>ε</i> and $`1-\varepsilon`$ differ by at most $`C\log(1/(2\varepsilon))/(\log n)^2`$, for a universal constant <i>C</i>.

## F187-P01. Snaky in 21 Maker moves

https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Snaky-in-21-Maker-moves-September-25-2026/article.pdf

We prove that Maker can achieve the Snaky hexomino within 21 actual Maker moves against arbitrary legal Breaker play on the initially empty infinite square board. The same bound holds on a $`17\times17`$ square; in fact, Maker can confine its claims to a fixed 251-cell board.
