## F246. Cannon's conjecture.

Discipline: Group theory

Every word-hyperbolic group with boundary homeomorphic to <i>S</i><sup>2</sup> admits a proper cocompact isometric action on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel, proving Cannon's conjecture. Every torsion-free such group is therefore the fundamental group of a closed hyperbolic three-manifold. ([Lean](lean/docs/246.md))

## F251. Amenability, unitarizability, and strong Ulam stability.

Discipline: Group theory

Resolves Dixmier's problem for all discrete groups: amenability is equivalent to every uniformly bounded Hilbert-space representation being similar to a unitary representation. For countable discrete groups, amenability is also equivalent to strong Ulam stability: sufficiently accurate unitary approximate representations are uniformly close in operator norm to genuine representations on the same, possibly infinite-dimensional, Hilbert space. ([Lean](lean/docs/251.md))

## F254. Classifying spaces and geometric obstructions for Artin groups.

Discipline: Group theory

## F263. The ionization and generalized ionization conjectures.
