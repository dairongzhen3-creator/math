## F306. The purely cosmetic surgery conjecture.

Discipline: Topology

Distinct Dehn surgery slopes on a nontrivial smooth knot in <i>S</i><sup>3</sup> never produce orientation-preservingly homeomorphic manifolds, proving the purely cosmetic surgery conjecture. The statement includes the meridional slope.

## F307. Failure of rational injectivity for maximal coarse assembly.

Discipline: Topology

Constructs a uniformly discrete bounded-geometry space whose maximal coarse assembly map is not rationally injective. The example is a coarse disjoint union of finite connected graphs of uniformly bounded degree, with an infinite-order kernel class. A companion gives the analogous failure for reduced coarse assembly, disproving the rational coarse Novikov conjecture. ([Lean](lean/docs/307.md))

## F308. Finite Smith–Toda complexes at every height.

Discipline: Topology

For every <i>n</i> ≥ 0, constructs a finite Smith–Toda spectrum at a prime <i>p</i> depending on <i>n</i>, with Brown–Peterson homology $`BP_*/(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)`$ and the canonical comodule structure. Thus Smith–Toda complexes exist at every height when the prime may vary; an explicit example realizes $`V(4)`$ at <i>p</i> = 1009.

## F309. The Kervaire invariant problem at the prime three.

Discipline: Topology

Resolves the odd-primary Kervaire invariant problem at the prime three: exactly the standard classes with indices 0, 2, and 3 survive in the mod-three Adams spectral sequence, in stems 10, 106, and 322. Each surviving detection coset contains an element of exact additive order three.

## F310. Quillen's conjecture in rational homology.

Discipline: Topology

Proves the rational-homology form of Quillen's conjecture for every finite group and every prime. If the largest normal <i>p</i>-subgroup of <i>G</i> is trivial, the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian <i>p</i>-subgroups has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology and is therefore not contractible.

- F310-P01: [Rational homology and Quillen's conjecture](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Rational-homology-and-Quillens-conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## F311. The Hovey–Strickland and Chai conjectures.

Discipline: Topology

Proves Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture for Lubin–Tate deformation rings over finite residue fields, at every prime and positive height <i>n</i>. Through the implication of Barthel–Heard–Naumann, this proves the Hovey–Strickland conjecture: dualizable $`K(n)`$-local spectra have exactly $`n+2`$ thick tensor ideals, and their Balmer spectrum is a chain of $`n+1`$ points.

- F311-P01: [Stabilizer orbits and thick tensor ideals of dualizable K(n)-local spectra](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Stabilizer-Orbits-and-Thick-Tensor-Ideals-of-Dualizable-Kn-Local-Spectra-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## F313. Finite generation for the $`K(n)`$-local sphere.

Discipline: Topology

Answers the degreewise finiteness question of Hovey and Hovey–Strickland: every homotopy group of the $`K(n)`$-local sphere is a finitely generated ℤ<sub><i>p</i></sub>-module, for every prime, positive height and integer degree. The same conclusion holds after $`K(n)`$-localizing any finite <i>p</i>-local spectrum.

## F314. Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss.

Discipline: Topology

Determines the optimal chromatic loss from geometric <i>H</i>-fixed points to geometric <i>G</i>-fixed points for every subgroup <i>H</i> of a finite <i>p</i>-group <i>G</i>. At every nonnegative height, the loss equals the shortest subnormal-chain length from <i>H</i> to <i>G</i> with cyclic quotients. Each quotient counts once regardless of order, and finite spectra witness sharpness.

- F314-P01: [Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026.pdf)

## F315. The four-dimensional Singer conjecture.

Discipline: Topology

Proves that the <i>L</i><sup>2</sup>-Betti numbers of the universal cover of every closed connected aspherical topological four-manifold vanish outside degree two. More generally, the same conclusion holds for every finite connected aspherical integral Poincaré complex of formal dimension four, proving the four-dimensional Singer conjecture in this wider class.

- F315-P01: [The Singer conjecture in dimension four](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-Singer-conjecture-in-dimension-four-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## F318. Chromatic splitting: filtrations and counterexamples.

Discipline: Topology

Disproves strong chromatic splitting at height three for primes <i>p</i> ≥ 5, and weak splitting for the derived <i>p</i>-completed sphere at heights <i>p</i> (<i>p</i> ≥ 5) and $`p+1`$ (<i>p</i> ≥ 7). Nevertheless, for <i>n</i> ≥ 1 and $`p\gt n+1`$, the overlap $`L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge`$ admits a $`2^n`$-stage filtration by the predicted localized-sphere pieces. At height three and prime three, even finite assembly from such pieces fails in the category of $`E(2)`$-local modules over the derived completed sphere.

## F319. Counterexamples to finite generation at chromatic height two.

Discipline: Topology

Refutes the Hahn–Wilson conjecture at chromatic height two. For every sufficiently large prime <i>p</i>, constructs a connective <i>p</i>-complete spectrum of exact fp-type two that cannot be built from completed $`\mathrm{BP}\langle2\rangle`$ by finitely many sums, shifts, cones and retracts. The examples nevertheless satisfy the finite and telescopic localization comparisons.

Discipline: Topology

## F321. A counterexample to Wall's finite D(2) problem.

Discipline: Topology

Constructs a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex whose universal cover has no integral homology above degree two and whose third cohomology vanishes for every local coefficient module, but which has no finite two-dimensional homotopy model. This disproves Wall's finite D(2) conjecture; the example has infinite fundamental group.

## F322. Tingley’s sphere-isometry problem.

Discipline: Functional analysis

Resolves Tingley's problem: every surjective isometry between the unit spheres of nonzero real Banach spaces extends uniquely to a surjective real-linear isometry of the whole spaces. No dimension restriction is imposed, so the metric geometry of the unit sphere determines the Banach space up to linear isometry. ([Lean](lean/docs/322.md))

## F323. Independence of the separable quotient problem.

Discipline: Functional analysis

Establishes, relative to the consistency of a measurable cardinal, that the separable quotient problem is independent of ZFC. The assertion that every infinite-dimensional Banach space has a separable infinite-dimensional quotient can hold for all real and complex Banach spaces, whereas the continuum hypothesis yields counterexamples over both fields. ([Lean](lean/docs/323.md))

## F325. The complete Crouzeix conjecture.

Discipline: Functional analysis

Resolves the complete Crouzeix conjecture: for every bounded operator <i>A</i> on a complex Hilbert space and every finite matrix-valued polynomial <i>P</i>, one has $`\lVert P[A]\rVert\le2\sup_{z\in W(A)}\lVert P(z)\rVert`$, where $`W(A)`$ is the numerical range. The constant 2 is sharp, independent of the matrix size, and valid in infinite dimensions. ([Lean](lean/docs/325.md))
