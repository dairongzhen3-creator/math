## F244. The Partition Principle does not imply Choice.

Discipline: Mathematical logic

## F245. Weak normalization implies strong normalization in pure type systems.

Discipline: Mathematical logic

Proves that weak normalization implies strong normalization for every pure type system: if every legal expression in every valid context has a <i>β</i>-normal form, every <i>β</i>-reduction sequence terminates.
