## F125. The metric k-median approximation threshold and recovery.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

## F126. Exponential semidefinite complexity of perfect matching.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Proves that every exact semidefinite lift of the perfect matching polytope has exponential size, answering Rothvoss's polynomial-size lift question negatively. The bound holds even for the positive semidefinite rank of its odd-cut slack matrix after any fixed shift $`0\lt \rho\lt 1`$, allowing arbitrary real positive semidefinite factors. ([Lean](lean/docs/126.md))

## F127. Average sensitivity of polynomial threshold functions.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Proves that a degree-at-most-<i>d</i> polynomial threshold function on the uniform <i>n</i>-dimensional Boolean cube has average sensitivity at most $`8d\sqrt n`$, uniformly for $`1\le d\le n`$. Average sensitivity counts expected output changes under single-bit flips. This establishes the asymptotic Gotsman–Linial conjecture, allowing polynomial zeros with $`\mathop{\mathrm{sign}}\nolimits (0)=1`$. ([Lean](lean/docs/127.md))

- F127-P01: [Average sensitivity of polynomial threshold functions](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Average-Sensitivity-of-Polynomial-Threshold-Functions-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## F130. Exact Fourier transforms below $`n\log n`$.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Gives a deterministic length-<i>n</i> discrete Fourier transform algorithm using $`O(n(\log n)^{1-\delta})`$ operations for every <i>n</i>, with explicit $`\delta=10^{-13}`$. ## F131. Rapid mixing of graph switches for every degree sequence.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

## F132. A superquadratic separation of sensitivity and block sensitivity.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Constructs total Boolean functions with block sensitivity $`\mathop{\mathrm{bs}}\nolimits (f)\ge s(f)^\alpha`$ for a fixed <i>α</i> &gt; 2, disproving the quadratic strengthening of the Sensitivity Conjecture. Here $`s(f)`$ counts influential individual-bit flips, while block sensitivity allows disjoint groups of bits to change together. ([Lean](lean/docs/132.md))

## F133. The computational complexity of Weisfeiler–Leman refinement.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Proves unconditional $`n^{\Omega(k)}`$ deterministic time lower bounds for joint and separate <i>k</i>-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence, for sufficiently large fixed <i>k</i> in the specified sequential adjacency-matrix models. With dimension as input, joint equivalence is EXPTIME-complete even on subcubic graphs; deciding whether refinement identifies a graph is also EXPTIME-complete. ([Lean](lean/docs/133.md))

## F134. Generalized star height at most three.

## F135. Homogeneous depth-five lower bounds for iterated matrix multiplication.

## F136. A quasilinear PCP theorem for PPAD.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Resolves the quasilinear PCP-for-PPAD conjecture. An End-of-Line instance of length <i>N</i> reduces to numerical circuit constraints of total length $`N(\log N)^{O(1)}`$ such that any polynomially encoded rational assignment satisfying all but a fixed fraction to fixed accuracy yields an endpoint solution. Such assignments always exist, giving robust local verification with only quasilinear size overhead.

## F137. One-tape time simulation in two-fifths-power space.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

Determines the halting and finite-control outcome of a fixed deterministic one-writable-tape machine up to time <i>T</i> using $`O(T^{2/5}\log^C(T+2))`$ space, improving the square-root exponent. Heads move at most one cell per step; finitely many read-only input heads are allowed. Initial contents are independent of <i>T</i>, and contents and input symbols have polylogarithmic-space access. Simulation time is unrestricted.

## F139. Subpolynomial query complexity for log-concave sampling.

Discipline: Theoretical computer science

For <i>C</i><sup>2</sup> potentials with a supplied minimizer and $`I\preceq\nabla^2V\preceq2I`$, proves that sampling within total variation 1/10 requires only $`C_\varepsilon d^\varepsilon`$ exact value-and-gradient queries for every fixed <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. The bound holds on every run, with unrestricted computation between queries. A logarithmic lower bound also holds, so the optimal power-law exponent in this oracle model is zero.
