## F001. Milne’s rationality conjecture and algebraic specialization.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves Milne's rationality conjecture for abelian varieties over $`\overline{\mathbb Q}`$ with good reduction: specialized Hodge classes pair rationally with complementary divisor products, independently of cohomology theory. Together with result 032, every specialized Hodge class is represented by a single rational algebraic cycle simultaneously in all prime-to-<i>p</i> and crystalline realizations, for every residue characteristic <i>p</i>.

## F011. Prime-factor statistics of $`p-1`$.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves that the normalized ordered logarithms of the prime factors of $`p-1`$, counted with multiplicity, converge jointly to the Poisson–Dirichlet law $`\mathrm{PD}(1)`$ as <i>p</i> ranges uniformly over primes up to <i>x</i> and $`x\to\infty`$. This resolves the Ford–Konyagin–Luca conjecture. It also proves that infinitely many integers <i>n</i> have more than $`n^{1-\varepsilon}`$ totient preimages, for every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0.

## F014. Restricted geometric Langlands, global Arthur enhancements, and generic Ramanujan.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves the restricted geometric Langlands equivalence for connected reductive groups on smooth projective connected curves over $`\overline{\mathbb F}_q`$ under the four stated Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses. Over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristic <i>p</i> &gt; 0, the same conclusion holds assuming additionally that <i>p</i> is very good for the group and $`p\nmid |W_G|`$. Over global function fields, proves Ramanujan at every place for globally generic cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple exceptional groups, without characteristic or ramification-depth restrictions, and at every unramified place for cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple groups with a generic unramified component. Assuming the finite-level Ramanujan–Arthur decomposition, constructs global Arthur enhancements of occurring cuspidal excursion parameters for split connected semisimple groups at full finite level, recovering the given parameters by diagonal specialization on the entire Weil group, including inertia.
