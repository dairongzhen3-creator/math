## F021. A quadratic bound for Jacobsthal’s function.

Discipline: Number theory

Answers Jacobsthal's quadratic-bound question: every interval of $`Ck^2`$ consecutive integers contains an integer coprime to any prescribed positive integer with at most <i>k</i> distinct prime divisors, for an absolute constant <i>C</i>. The bound is uniform over prime sets and interval positions and removes the classical logarithmic loss. ([Lean](lean/docs/021.md))

## F022. The weak inhomogeneous Duffin–Schaeffer conjecture.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves that for every real shift <i>γ</i> and finite-valued $`\psi:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty)`$, divergence of $`\sum_q\phi(q)\psi(q)/q`$ implies $`\|qx-\gamma\|\lt \psi(q)`$ for infinitely many <i>q</i>, for almost every <i>x</i>. Here <i>ϕ</i> is Euler's totient and the norm is distance to the nearest integer. Numerators are unrestricted; no monotonicity or Diophantine condition on <i>γ</i> is needed.

## F023. Patterson's first moment for cubic Gauss sums.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves unconditionally the all-primary-prime form of Patterson’s first-moment asymptotic: normalized cubic Gauss sums over primary Eisenstein primes of norm at most <i>X</i>, including both conjugates, have an explicit positive main term of order $`X^{5/6}/\log X`$. Every fixed nonzero prime-angle Fourier mode has smaller order. ([Lean](lean/docs/023.md))

## F024. An asymptotic formula for the number of totients.

Discipline: Number theory

Gives an asymptotic equivalent for the number $`V(x)`$ of distinct totient values up to <i>x</i>, with a positive bounded phase-dependent factor determined by convergent arithmetic approximations. In particular, $`V(cx)/V(x)\to c`$ for every fixed <i>c</i> &gt; 0, answering Erdős and Hall’s scaling question. ([Lean](lean/docs/024.md))

## F025. Short Egyptian fractions.

Discipline: Number theory

Every rational $`a/b`$ with $`1\le a\lt b`$ is a sum of $`O(\log\log b)`$ distinct positive unit fractions. The worst-case minimum number of terms has the same order, resolving Erdős’s conjecture on short Egyptian fractions. ([Lean](lean/docs/025.md))

## F026. Positive lower density of large prime gaps.

Discipline: Number theory

For every fixed <i>C</i> &gt; 0, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed $`C\log p_n`$, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on <i>C</i>. Consequently, the indices where $`p_n/n`$ increases have positive lower density, answering Erdős and Prachar. ([Lean](lean/docs/026.md))

## F027. Potential integral density on curve character varieties.

Discipline: Number theory

Resolves the determinant-one curve case of Litt's integral-density question. For every smooth connected complex algebraic curve and every rank, integral points become Zariski dense in every component of its SL<sub><i>r</i></sub> character variety over the full ring of integers of one number field. Prescribed quasi-unipotent boundary conjugacy classes are allowed, including nonsemisimple classes.

## F028. Uniformly bounded components of Gaussian-prime graphs.

Discipline: Number theory

Proves the Gaussian moat conjecture: no infinite walk through distinct Gaussian primes can have uniformly bounded steps. More strongly, for every distance bound <i>D</i>, the graph joining Gaussian primes at distance at most <i>D</i> has uniformly bounded finite component sizes, depending only on <i>D</i>, including primes on the coordinate axes. ([Lean](lean/docs/028.md))
